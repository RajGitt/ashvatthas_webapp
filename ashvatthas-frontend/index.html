Return & Exchange Policy

7-Day Hassle-Free Returns

1. The Policy Window

You have 7 days from the date of delivery to request a return or an exchange.

2. Condition for Return

To maintain our premium standards, the item must be:

  • Unworn & Unwashed — No stains, odors (perfume/deodorant), or signs of wear.
  • Original Packaging — All tags, collar stays, and clips must be intact.
  • Non-Returnable — Custom-made (monogrammed) items or "Final Sale" pieces.

3. The Process

  • Reverse Pickup — We will arrange a courier pickup from your address within 24–48 hours of your request.
  • Quality Check — Once the item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, we process your request.

4. Refunds

Prepaid Orders: Refunded to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
COD Orders: Refunded via Store Credit or a Razorpay Payout link (Bank/UPI).
